Procedures

Our Services

Every procedure listed below is performed or directly supervised by Dr Kueh Nai Siong. Suitability is decided only after a personal examination and discussion — not from a website.

Facial Plastic Surgery Body Contouring Breast Surgery Intimate Surgery Reconstructive Surgery Fat Grafting & Rejuvenation Aesthetic Minor Procedures
Please note: this page lists the procedures available at the clinic. It is not a recommendation, and it does not describe outcomes, prices or suitability. Those are discussed individually during consultation, as required for any surgical procedure.
01

Facial Plastic Surgery

Surgery of the eyelids, nose, ears and facial contour. Facial procedures are millimetre work — small differences in technique change the result, so planning is done on your own anatomy rather than a template.

  • Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)
  • Double Eyelid Surgery
  • Eyebag Removal
  • Ptosis Correction
  • Brow Lift
  • Forehead Lift
  • Face Lift
  • Neck Lift
  • Rhinoplasty (Nose Surgery)
  • Alarplasty
  • Facial Contouring Surgery
  • Facial Implants
  • Autologous Fat Graft to Face
  • Dimple Creation
  • Otoplasty & Torn Earlobe Repair
02

Body Contouring

Reshaping of the abdomen, arms and thighs, including after significant weight loss or pregnancy. Contouring surgery removes or redistributes tissue; it is not a treatment for obesity and does not replace weight management.

  • Liposuction
  • Abdominoplasty
  • Mini Tummy Tuck
  • Arm Lift (Brachioplasty)
  • Thigh Lift
03

Breast Surgery

Cosmetic and reconstructive breast surgery for women and men, including reconstruction following mastectomy. Implant and fat-grafting options each have different trade-offs, which are explained fully before any decision is made.

  • Breast Augmentation
  • Implants & Fat Grafting
  • Breast Lift (Mastopexy)
  • Breast Reduction
  • Male Breast Reduction (Gynaecomastia)
  • Mastectomy & Reconstruction
  • Nipple Plasty
  • Areola Plasty
04

Intimate Surgery

Discreet consultations for functional and aesthetic concerns, including changes following childbirth. Privacy is respected at every stage, and a female chaperone is available on request.

  • Vaginoplasty
  • Labiaplasty
05

Reconstructive Surgery

Restoring form and function after birth difference, trauma, burns or cancer. This is the foundation of Dr Kueh's training at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, and remains a core part of the practice. Referrals from other doctors are welcome.

  • Cleft Lip & Cleft Palate Surgery
  • Skin Cancer Surgery
  • Burn Injury & Burn Reconstruction
  • Scar Revision
  • Reconstructive Microsurgery
06

Fat Grafting & Rejuvenation

Fat is harvested from one area of your body, processed, and transferred to another to restore volume. Because the tissue is your own, there is no implant material involved — though a proportion of grafted fat is reabsorbed, and more than one session is sometimes needed.

  • Autologous Fat Transfer
  • Facial Rejuvenation
  • Fat Grafting to Breasts
  • Lip Enhancement
  • Gluteal Fat Grafting
07

Aesthetic Minor Procedures

Non-surgical and minor treatments carried out in clinic, usually with little or no downtime. All injectable treatments are administered by a registered medical practitioner.

  • Thread Lift
  • Botulinum Toxin (Botox)
  • Hyaluronic Acid Fillers
  • Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP)
  • Mesotherapy
  • Mole Removal
What to expect

From enquiry to recovery

Consultation

A private appointment with Dr Kueh. Examination, medical history, and a frank discussion of options, risks and realistic outcomes.

Planning & preparation

A written plan with costs, the venue for surgery, pre-operative tests where needed, and a cooling-off period to consider it.

Surgery & follow-up

Your procedure at the clinic or an accredited hospital, followed by scheduled review visits until healing is complete.
